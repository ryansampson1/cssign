# FastSigns (store 2041) price history: Ryan's own orders, 2016–2026

This covers 92 invoices pulled from the CoreBridge client portal. All prices are pre-tax, and FastSigns charged 7.0% sales tax on recent invoices.

## Standard site sign: 4×8 panel + 2 painted 4×4×10 posts + install

| Period | Panel | Posts (pair) | Install | Typical total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2016–2018 | MDO $295 | $50 | $75 (1 hr) | **$420** |
| 2019–2021 | MDO $295 | $50 | $100 (some $150–300) | **$445** |
| Late 2021–early 2022 | MDO $300 | $50 | $100 | **$450** |
| Aug 2022–2023 | MDO $350 | $50 | $125–150 | **$525–550** |
| Oct 2025 | ACM 4×8 $450 | $100 (black) | $150 | **$700** |
| Feb 2026 | 6mm ACM $400 | $60 | $150 | **$610** |

The standard package went from $420 to $610–700, up 45–67% in nine years. Most of the 2023–2025 jump came from the switch to ACM and higher post prices.

## Other line items

- **Reface an existing MDO panel:** $160 from 2016 to 2020. Riders (1×8) ran $55–95.
- **Removal and storage:** $65 (2016). Repair: $65.
- **Grand-format banners, 9′×40′:** $720 each (2019) and $792 each (2021), about $2.00–2.20/SF. Banner installs cost $300–400. A pylon banner with bucket-truck install in 2025 was $320.65 + $525.
- **Install:** billed as "1 hour" at $75 through 2018, then a flat $100–150. Multi-sign jobs got one install charge.

## Where the price book lands (defaults, Tampa Zone 1)

- **Standard package:** 4×8 ACM double-sided $425 + post package $95 + install $135 = **$655**. That is $45 under FastSigns' Oct 2025 ACM invoice and $45 over the Feb 2026 one, at a 50% gross margin on loaded cost.
- **MDO 4×8 package:** $685. Their last MDO price was in 2023 ($550).
- **Reface:** $225 + install. Their last reface price was in 2020 ($160 + install).
- **Removal:** free for Program and Builder accounts. FastSigns bills it separately.
